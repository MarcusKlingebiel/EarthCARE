HOW TO LAND AN AIRCRAFT

1. Click the blue arrival aircraft (or its strip on the right)
2. Type h 270 to point it toward the runway from the east
3. Descend: type a 030 (= 3000 ft) while still 10–20nm out
4. Type app 27L — aircraft intercepts the dashed ILS line
and auto-lands. ↘ = intercepting, ▼ = on final.
Runways: 27L · 27R · 09L · 09R · 18 · 36 · 14 · 32
Also works: app 27 (= 27L), app 9 (= 09R)

Arrivals (blue) must land. Departures (orange) route to edge waypoints.
Keep 3nm / 1000ft separation between all aircraft.
