HOW TO LAND AN AIRCRAFT

1. Click the arrival aircraft (or its strip on the right)
2. Type h 270 to point it toward the runway from the east
3. Descend: type a 030 (= 3000 ft) while still 10–20nm out
4. Type app 27L — aircraft intercepts the dashed ILS line
and auto-lands. ↘ = intercepting, ▼ = on final.

Arrivals (blue strip) must land. Departures (orange strip) route to edge waypoints.
Keep 3nm / 1000ft separation. Blip color = altitude band.
⚡ MAYDAY aircraft need landing within 3 min — priority landing!
Every 5 completions advances level — faster spawns, more traffic.
