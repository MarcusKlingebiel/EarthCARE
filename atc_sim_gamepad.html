HOW TO LAND AN AIRCRAFT

1. D↑↓ to highlight the arrival, A to select it
2. RB → HDG, adjust to point toward runway, A to send
3. RB → ALT, set FL030 (3000ft) while 10–20nm out, A to send
4. Y to jump to ILS, D↑↓ pick runway, A to clear for ILS
↘ = intercepting · ▼ = on final · auto-lands!

Arrivals (blue strip) must land. Departures (orange strip) route to edge waypoints.
